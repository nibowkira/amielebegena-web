Why the Begena is Called the Harp of King David

Master Artisan Dawit May 24, 2026 7 min read

In the hearts of traditional musicians and believers, the Begena is known by no title more sacred than the Harp of King David. But what is the origin of this profound association?

The Biblical Narrative in Ethiopian Tradition

In the Old Testament, King David plays his harp before King Saul to drive away tormenting spirits and bring divine peace. Ethiopian Orthodox Christian tradition maintains that the design and spiritual purpose of the Begena were preserved directly from David's royal instruments.

Liturgical Use in the Orthodox Church

During Great Lent and other sacred seasons, Ethiopian monks and devoted laypersons spend hours strumming the Begena while chanting spiritual poems reflecting on mortality, repentance, and divine grace.
