Traditional Ethiopian Musical Instruments: Complete Overview

Amiele Heritage Team May 5, 2026 9 min read

Ethiopia boasts one of the world's most unique and ancient musical traditions, anchored by a rich family of acoustic instruments crafted from natural materials.

Key Ethiopian Instruments

  • Begena: The sacred 10-string harp of King David.
  • Kirar: The versatile 5-string folk lyre.
  • Masinko: The single-string bowed fiddle.
  • Kebero: The ceremonial double-headed church drum.
